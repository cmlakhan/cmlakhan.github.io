## About Me

I am a Research Scientist in the Knowles Lab where I am broadly interested in utilizing genomic deep learning models to better understand Alzheimer's disease genetics.  I am active in the NIH Alzheimer's Disease Sequencing Project consortium, where I work the the broader community to elucidate the functional mechanism of common and rare AD risk variants.

## Research Interests
