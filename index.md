## About Me

I am a Research Scientist in the [Knowles Lab](https://daklab.github.io/) at the [New York Genome Center](https://www.nygenome.org/) where I am broadly interested in utilizing genomic deep learning models to better understand Alzheimer's disease genetics.  I am an active memver of the Artificial Intelligence/Machine Learning working group within the [NIH Alzheimer's Disease Sequencing Project Consortium](https://adsp.niagads.org/), where I work with the broader community to elucidate the functional mechanism of common and rare AD risk variants.

## Research Interests

- **Statistical Genetics**
- **Machine Learning** 
- **Alzheimer's Disease**
